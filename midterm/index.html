my yorkshire terrier

The Yorkshire Terrier, also known as a Yorkie, is a British breed of toy dog of terrier type. It is among the smallest of the terriers and indeed of all dog breeds, with a weight of no more than 3.2 kg (7 lb). It originated in the nineteenth century in the English county of Yorkshire, after which it is named. The coat is tan on the head and dark steel-grey on the body; no other colour is accepted by either The Kennel Club or the Fédération Cynologique Internationale.It is playful and energetic, usually kept as a companion dog. It has contributed to the development of other breeds including the Silky Terrier, and also to cross-breeds such as the Yorkipoo. (i took this info from Wikipedia by the way). Lulu is my Yorkshire Terrier. She is almost 10 years old now, my poor dog is getting old! Despite what Wikipedia says about Yorkie's behaviours, my dog breaks all those expectations. When she finally this year came face to face with a mouse, she was AFRAID of the mouse and ran away from it. She is also extremely noisy when she sleeps even though she is very tiny. As I was making this website, she was snoring very loudly. Ever since I got her, she prefers to sleep over playing with toys. She behaves more like a cat than a dog. If I ever were to get another dog in the future, I would 100% get a Yorkshire Terrier.

facts about yorkshire terriers

  • Yorkies transitioned from a working dogs into fashionable lapdogs for Victorian ladies.
  • Longhaired, their hair just keeps growing!
  • Descends from the Skye Terrier and extinct Paisley Terrier
  • They're hypoallergenic!
